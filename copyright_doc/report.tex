\section{Legal Issues and Considerations}
For an official release of the Anticipate digital touchpoint, several legal hurdles must be navigated. Primarily, the application utilizes numerous open-source libraries under the MIT and Apache 2.0 licenses, which require the inclusion of original copyright notices and license texts within the distribution. 

Furthermore, the educational content references trademarked entities such as \textbf{MoneySavingExpert} and \textbf{Martin Lewis}. While used here for educational context within a prototype, an official release would require explicit permission or a licensing agreement to avoid trademark infringement or passing off. 

Additionally, as the application provides financial guidance (e.g., pension advice, debt strategies), it must comply with \textbf{Financial Conduct Authority (FCA)} regulations in the UK. This is critical to ensure the platform does not cross the line into providing unregulated financial advice, which could carry significant legal penalties. 

\section{Note for Future Development Teams}
Future teams continuing work on Anticipate must adhere to the following legal and structural constraints:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{License Compliance:} Ensure all third-party code maintains its original license headers. Use auditing tools to verify dependencies before any production build.
    \item \textbf{Trademark Audit:} Before any public deployment, audit the educational content and UI strings to remove or seek permission for trademarked names and logos.
    \item \textbf{Regulatory Review:} Consulting with a legal expert on UK financial regulation is mandatory if the "Sage" AI or lesson content is expanded to provide specific, personalized financial recommendations.
\end{itemize}
