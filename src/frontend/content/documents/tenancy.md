## Key terms
- **Property:** 47B Ferndale Road, London SW9 8AN
- **Term:** 12 months (1 Jun 2025 – 31 May 2026)
- **Monthly rent:** £1,650.00, payable in advance
- **Security deposit:** £1,903.84 (5 weeks' rent)
- **Deposit scheme:** Tenancy Deposit Scheme (TDS)
- **Break clause:** 6 months — either party, 2 months' notice

## Terms & Conditions
1. **Tenancy type:** This agreement creates an Assured Shorthold Tenancy (AST) under the Housing Act 1988. At the end of the fixed term, the tenancy will become a periodic (rolling) tenancy unless either party serves notice to end it.
2. **Deposit protection:** The landlord will protect the security deposit of £1,903.84 with the Tenancy Deposit Scheme (TDS) within 30 days of receipt.
3. **Guarantor obligations:** The guarantor agrees to be jointly and severally liable for all rental payments and obligations under this tenancy should the tenant default.
4. **Condition & inventory:** An inventory and check-in report will be prepared prior to move-in. The property must be returned in the same condition, allowing for fair wear and tear.
5. **Ending the tenancy:** After the fixed term, the landlord may serve a Section 21 notice to request possession, requiring at least 2 months' notice. The tenant must give 1 month's notice in writing to end a periodic tenancy.
