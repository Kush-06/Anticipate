# Mortgage ESIS (European Standardised Information Sheet)

## Key Details
- **Lender:** Meridian Bank
- **Property Value:** £300,000
- **Loan Amount:** £240,000
- **LTV (Loan-to-Value):** 80%
- **Interest Rate Type:** 2-year Fixed
- **Initial Rate:** 4.75%
- **Product Fee:** £999 (can be added to loan)
- **APRC:** 5.8%

## Important Terms
1. **Nature of the loan:** This is a residential mortgage secured against your property. If you do not keep up with repayments, your home may be repossessed.
2. **Fixed period:** For the first 2 years, your interest rate is fixed at 4.75%. Your monthly payments will not change during this time, even if the Bank of England base rate changes.
3. **Early Repayment Charges (ERC):** If you pay off the mortgage or switch to a different product during the 2-year fixed period, you will be charged an ERC.
4. **Product fee:** You can pay the £999 fee upfront or add it to the total loan amount. If added to the loan, you will pay interest on this fee for the life of the mortgage.
5. **After the fixed period:** Your mortgage will automatically switch to the lender's Standard Variable Rate (SVR), which is currently 7.5%. This can change at any time.
