# Pension Welcome Letter

## Key Details
- **Provider:** Nest Pensions
- **Scheme Type:** Defined Contribution
- **Employer Contribution:** 3%
- **Your Contribution:** 5%
- **Status:** Auto-enrolled

## Important Terms
1. **Auto-enrolment:** You have been automatically enrolled into the workplace pension scheme as you meet the eligibility criteria (earnings over £10,000/year and aged between 22 and State Pension age).
2. **Contributions:** Contributions are taken from your gross pay before tax, meaning you get tax relief. Your employer matches your contributions, effectively adding free money to your retirement pot.
3. **Investment:** Your pension contributions are invested in the Nest Retirement Date Fund (2060). This fund manages risk automatically, becoming less volatile as you approach your target retirement year.
4. **Opt-out:** You have the right to opt out of the scheme within one month of joining. If you do, you will stop receiving employer contributions and may lose out on long-term growth.
5. **Portability:** You can take your pension pot with you if you change jobs or transfer it to another provider, though you should seek financial advice before doing so.

---
This document provides details of your workplace pension. Pension values can go down as well as up. You should monitor your pension regularly.
