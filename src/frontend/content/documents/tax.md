## Important Terms
1. **SA302:** This document is your official tax calculation from HMRC for your self-assessment tax return. It summarizes your total income, tax paid, and any remaining balance.
2. **Taxable Income:** The total amount of income on which you are liable to pay tax, after deducting any tax-free allowances.
3. **Payments on Account:** If your tax bill is over £1,000, HMRC may require you to make 'Payments on Account' for the following tax year. These are advance payments towards your next bill, due in January and July.
4. **Balance Due:** If you have a balance due, you must pay this to HMRC by 31 January following the end of the tax year.
5. **Record Keeping:** You must keep all records used to complete your tax return for at least 5 years after the 31 January submission deadline.
