## Important Terms
1. **Plan 2:** This plan applies to most students who started university in England or Wales between 1 September 2012 and 31 July 2023. Repayments are 9% of earnings above the threshold.
2. **Interest Rate:** Interest is charged from the day your first payment is made until your loan is paid in full or cancelled. The interest rate is currently linked to the Retail Price Index (RPI) plus up to 3%.
3. **Threshold:** You only start repaying your loan when your income is above the threshold for your plan. The threshold for Plan 2 is currently £27,295 per year.
4. **Repayments:** Repayments are automatically deducted from your salary through the UK tax system (PAYE). If you are self-employed, you make repayments through your self-assessment tax return.
5. **Loan Write-off:** Your student loan will be written off (cancelled) 30 years after you were first due to make repayments, regardless of the remaining balance.
