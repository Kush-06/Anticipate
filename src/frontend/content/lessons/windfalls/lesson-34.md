# Lesson 34: The 30-Day Pause

Receiving a financial windfall — whether a surprise inheritance, a work bonus, a Premium Bond win, or a property sale — is exciting. But a sudden influx of cash can also trigger anxiety, pressure from family, and a rush of dopamine that leads to impulsive decisions.

According to financial experts, the absolute best thing you can do when you receive a windfall is... **absolutely nothing**.

---

## 1. The Emotional Rollercoaster

Money is emotional. If your windfall is a bonus, you might feel an overwhelming urge to reward yourself with a luxury car or a lavish holiday. If it's an inheritance, the money is often tied to grief, which can cloud your judgement.

Taking a **30-day cooling-off period** allows your emotions to settle. It shifts your brain from impulsive, short-term thinking to logical, long-term planning. For life-changing sums (like a major inheritance), experts often recommend pausing for up to **six months** before buying property, quitting your job, or making massive investments.

---

## 2. Park It Safely (The £120,000 Rule)

During this pause, do not leave the money sitting in your current account where it's easily spent. Park it in a safe, easy-access savings account.

The **FSCS (Financial Services Compensation Scheme)** legally protects your money up to **£120,000 per person, per financial institution** if the bank goes bust.

| Windfall Amount | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Under £120,000 | Put it in the highest-paying easy-access savings account you can find |
| Over £120,000 | Split the money across multiple banking groups to ensure every penny is protected |

> The FSCS does offer temporary high-balance protection up to £1 million for up to six months for specific life events (house sale, inheritance), but splitting it is the safest long-term habit.

---

## 3. Build a "Fun Fund"

The 30-Day Pause is not about depriving yourself. Carve out a small percentage — **5% to 10%** — as guilt-free "fun money" right away. Spend this on a holiday, a nice dinner, or a gadget.

By satisfying the immediate urge to spend, you make it much easier to lock away the remaining 90% for 30 days while you build a sensible plan.
