# Lesson 10: The Hidden Costs of Buying

Your deposit is just the entry ticket. To actually buy a house, you need several thousand pounds in liquid cash to cover legal fees, taxes, and checks. **Lenders will not let you add these costs to your mortgage** — you must have them sitting in your bank account ready to go.

---

## 1. Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT)

Stamp Duty is a government property tax. In April 2025, the generous pandemic-era thresholds were removed, making Stamp Duty a much bigger burden for first-time buyers in 2026.

**Current First-Time Buyer rules:**

| Property Value | Tax Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to £300,000 | 0% |
| £300,001 – £500,000 | 5% |
| Over £500,000 | Standard Stamp Duty rates (all first-time buyer relief lost) |

**Example:** Buy a house for £380,000. The first £300k is tax-free. You pay 5% on the remaining £80k. **Stamp Duty bill = £4,000**, paid in cash upon completion.

---

## 2. Conveyancing and Searches — £1,500 to £2,500

Your solicitor handles the legal transfer of the property. They also run **"Searches"** on the property via the local council and environmental agencies. These searches reveal hidden issues: planned motorways nearby, contaminated land, or whether the road outside is maintained by the council or residents.

---

## 3. Property Surveys — £400 to £1,200

A **Mortgage Valuation** is done by the bank to ensure they aren't lending £300k for a house worth £200k. It is not a survey of the property's condition. You must hire an independent surveyor to protect yourself.

| Survey Type | Cost | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Level 2 Homebuyer Report** | ~£500 | Standard, modern homes in reasonable condition. Traffic-light rating system highlighting major structural flaws, damp, and roof issues. |
| **Level 3 Structural Survey** | ~£1,000 | Properties over 50 years old, visibly run-down, or heavily altered. A forensic examination of the building's fabric. |

---

## 4. Mortgage Arrangement Fees — £0 to £1,499

The best mortgage interest rates usually come with an upfront "product fee." You can usually add this fee to your total mortgage loan rather than paying it in cash — but doing so means **you will pay interest on that fee for the next 25+ years**.
