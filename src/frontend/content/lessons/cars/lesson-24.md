# Lesson 24: Car Finance Demystified

In the UK today, over 80% of new cars are bought using dealership finance. But the glossy brochures and attractive monthly figures often hide complex financial structures. Choosing the wrong product can cost you thousands of pounds or leave you trapped in an agreement that doesn't suit your lifestyle.

---

## 1. Personal Contract Purchase (PCP): Paying for Depreciation

PCP is the most heavily pushed finance product in UK showrooms. It is designed to get you into a more expensive car for a lower monthly payment. But there is a massive catch: **you are not buying the car**.

**The Mechanism:**
Instead of borrowing the full £20,000 the car is worth, the finance company estimates what the car will be worth at the end of your contract (say, £8,000). This is called the **Guaranteed Minimum Future Value (GMFV)**. You only borrow and pay off the difference (£12,000), plus interest on the whole amount.

**End of Contract — Three Options:**
1. **Hand it back:** Walk away with nothing (assuming no damage or excess mileage)
2. **Trade it in:** Use any equity as a deposit on your next PCP deal (this is what dealers want, trapping you in permanent car payments)
3. **Buy it:** Pay the final "balloon payment" (the £8,000 GMFV) to finally own the car

**The Danger Zones:**
PCP contracts come with strict annual mileage limits (e.g., 8,000 miles/year). Exceed your limit and you face severe penalty fees, often 10p to 15p per excess mile.

---

## 2. Hire Purchase (HP): The Path to Ownership

HP is the traditional route if you actually want to own the car at the end of the term.

**The Mechanism:**
Put down a deposit (usually 10%), and the remaining value of the car is divided equally over 1 to 5 years, plus interest. Make your final payment, pay a small admin fee (~£100–200), and the car is **100% yours**.

**The Benefits:**
- No mileage limits
- No wear-and-tear penalties
- No balloon payment

**The Trade-off:**
Because you are paying off the full value of the car, monthly payments will be **significantly higher** than a PCP quote for the exact same car.

---

## 3. The Standard Bank Loan: The Cash Buyer Disguise

Dealerships earn commission on their own finance products. But you don't have to use them.

**The Mechanism:**
Borrow from your bank. The money goes into your current account. You walk into the dealership and buy the car outright as a **cash buyer**. You repay the bank over a set term.

**The Benefits:**
- You own the car from day one
- If you need to sell, you can sell privately and keep the difference (with PCP or HP, you cannot legally sell without the finance company's permission)
- Bank loans often have lower APRs, especially with an excellent credit score

---

## Summary Comparison

| Feature | PCP | HP | Bank Loan |
|---|---|---|---|
| What do payments cover? | Car's depreciation | Car's full value | Borrowed cash |
| Monthly cost | Lowest | Higher | Varies by rate |
| Who owns the car? | Finance company | You (after final payment) | You (from day one) |
| Mileage restrictions? | Yes, strictly enforced | No | No |
| Large final bill? | Yes, if you want to keep it | No | No |
