# Lesson 6: Budgeting for Bills

**The True Cost of Living Alone**

Rent is just the baseline. When you move out, you become responsible for running the property. A major trap for first-time renters is failing to budget for monthly utilities.

---

## Core Bills Breakdown

| Bill Type | How It Works | Pro-Tip for Renters |
|---|---|---|
| **Council Tax** | A local tax paying for rubbish collection, police, and street lighting. Paid in 10 or 12 monthly instalments. | If you live alone, claim your **25% Single Person Discount**. If your whole household is full-time students, you are **100% exempt**. |
| **Energy (Gas/Elec)** | You pay a daily "standing charge" just to be connected to the grid, plus the cost of energy you actually use. | Take a photo of the meters the exact day you move in. Otherwise you might be charged for the previous tenant's usage. |
| **Water** | Either a water meter (pay for exactly what you use) or unmetered (fixed rate based on property size). | If you have more bedrooms than people (e.g., 1 person in a 2-bed flat), a water meter is usually cheaper. |
| **Broadband** | Usually a fixed 12, 18, or 24-month contract. | 30–50 Mbps is plenty for one person streaming Netflix in 4K. Don't overpay for speed you won't use. |
| **TV Licence** | £169.50 a year. Required if you watch live TV on any channel or use BBC iPlayer. | If you strictly watch Netflix, YouTube, and non-BBC catch-up TV, you **legally do not need one**. |
